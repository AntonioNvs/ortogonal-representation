**Source:** https://www.sloansportsconference.com/research-paper-competition#Rules  
**Saved:** 2026-09-10  
**Target conference:** SSAC27 (abstracts due Oct 1, 2026)

This project targets submission to the **Other Sports** track (Formula 1).

---

## Competition format

1. **Abstract phase** — Authors submit abstracts; selected authors are invited to full manuscripts.
2. **Full manuscript phase** — Referees evaluate manuscripts; best submissions are invited to present; a separate set is invited to poster session.
3. **Conference phase**
   - **Presentations:** One winner per sports track plus one wildcard, judged on manuscript and presentation quality.
   - **Poster competition:** Independent of presentation finals; Best Poster via fan + judges vote.

## Open-source requirement

- All submissions must include a link to a **GitHub or other open-source repository**.
- Repository must include **data used** in the research (public or anonymized private/proprietary data).
- **Code is encouraged** but not strictly required; data sharing is mandatory.
- Anonymize personal information in any shared private data.

## Abstract guidelines

- **< 500 words** including title and body.
- Up to **two tables or figures combined** (e.g. 1 figure + 1 table).
- Required sections:
  - **Introduction** — research question and industry importance
  - **Methods** — statistical methods, data sources, collection procedures
  - **Results** — actual (not promised) results with relevant statistics
  - **Conclusion** — takeaway and impact on the sports industry

Abstracts are judged on **novelty, academic rigor, and impact**.

---

## Evaluation criteria

### Abstract and full paper

- **Novelty** — new models or challenges to existing beliefs
- **Academic rigor / validity** — sound, appropriate methodology
- **Reproducibility** — results replicable independently
- **Application** — practical or potential applications of insights

### Presentation finalists (additional)

- **Interest / impact** — significance of the question; benefits of the model or application

Final winner selection weighs **presentation heavily**, especially application and impact.

---

## Conflict of interest policy

Reviewers avoid evaluating work from prior collaborators, current/former students, same-organization colleagues, or other relationships that could bias review. Final reviews are blind to author names when possible.

---

## Rights and permissions

- Authors **retain ownership** and may publish after the conference.
- Upon submission, authors grant **42 Analytics** rights to make research available online/in print for conference use.
- Authors are responsible for third-party copyright permissions (data, tables, figures).
